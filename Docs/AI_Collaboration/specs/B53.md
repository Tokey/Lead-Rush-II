# B53 - Empirical Human Aim/Miss Pattern, Dual-Layer Pathfinding & Brain Component Breakdown

## 1. Goal and Problem Statement

`Lead Rush II` already models first-order human motor kinematics (`Fitts's Law`, Flash-Hogan minimum-jerk flicks, Harris-Wolpert signal-dependent noise, and recoil pull-down latency). However, comparing those pure isotropic math curves against real in-game human FPS telemetry reveals five systematic differences between idealized symmetric Gaussians and real human misses:
1. **Flick Endpoint Anisotropy & Undershoot Bias:** Real ballistic saccades exhibit ~2.0–2.5x larger variance **along** the flick axis ($\sigma_{\parallel}$) than **perpendicular** to it ($\sigma_{\perp}$), and tend to fall **short (undershoot)** by ~6–9% of large flick amplitudes before a secondary corrective submovement closes the remaining gap — only flipping toward overshoot under high panic/stress.
2. **Wrist/Forearm Horizontal-vs-Vertical Asymmetry:** Human mouse ergonomics decouple horizontal wrist yaw from vertical shoulder/finger pitch (`~1.55:1` horizontal-to-vertical error variance), producing a horizontal shoulder-height miss halo rather than a circular cloud.
3. **Visuomotor Target-Velocity Lag (`125 ms`):** Human visual motion estimation lags real-time target direction changes (`ADAD` counter-strafes) by ~`110–150 ms`, causing trailing-edge misses on sudden direction reversals rather than instant velocity lead.
4. **Early-Burst Climb + Late-Spray Lateral Sway:** Pro and amateur CS:GO/CS2 spray traces show early uncompensated vertical misses (`shots 2–4` before pull-down engages at `65–135 ms`) followed by horizontal bimanual weave around `2.5–3.2 Hz` once sustained spray fatigue builds.
5. **Artifact & Documentation Transparency (`https://claude.ai/artifact/NrTa76HJCAAFzydVUuD2Zo`):** The interactive explainer (`Docs/Bot/bot-brain.html` + `Docs/LR2_Bot_Explainer.md`) must document and simulate (a) every brain component (`BotPerception`, `BotBlackboard`, `TacticalBotBrain`, `BotController`, `AdaptiveDifficultyTracker`, `IBotDirectiveProvider`), (b) both pathfinding layers (`TacticalBotBrain` tactical scorer + `BotController` decoupled NavMesh coroutine/steering/corner-slicing/stuck recovery), and (c) the empirical human miss distribution.

## 3. Exact Model & Parameters Implemented in `BotController.cs`

1. **125 ms Low-Pass Perceived Velocity (`_perceivedTargetVel`):**
   $$\mathbf{v}_{\text{perc}}(t) = \text{Lerp}\!\left(\mathbf{v}_{\text{perc}}(t - \Delta t),\,\mathbf{v}_{\text{target}}(t),\,\frac{\Delta t}{0.125 + \Delta t}\right)$$
   Lead offset uses $\mathbf{v}_{\text{perc}} \cdot \text{leadTime}$ (`0.055 s` mean perceptual lead), producing realistic trailing-edge misses whenever an opponent sharply counter-strafes (`ADAD`).

2. **Anisotropic Longitudinal vs. Orthogonal Ballistic Flick Error ($\sigma_{\parallel} / \sigma_{\perp} \approx 2.27$):**
   Let $\hat{\mathbf{u}} = (\Delta\psi, \Delta\theta)/D$ be the flick unit vector and $\hat{\mathbf{n}} = (-\Delta\theta/D, \Delta\psi/D)$ its normal:
   $$\mu_{\parallel} = \text{Lerp}(-0.075,\, +0.035,\, \text{stress}) \cdot D, \qquad \varepsilon_{\parallel} = \mu_{\parallel} + \eta_1 \cdot \sigma_{\text{sdn}}, \qquad \varepsilon_{\perp} = \eta_2 \cdot 0.44\,\sigma_{\text{sdn}}$$
   where $\eta_{1,2} \in [-1, 1]$ scaled triangularly/clamped gives a stretched error ellipse along the saccade trajectory (classic human flick undershoot when calm, tipping toward overshoot under heavy fire).

3. **Horizontal Wrist-Yaw vs. Vertical Pitch Anisotropy (`1.55:1`):**
   Tracking noise and Perlin tremor apply `1.15x` horizontal multiplier vs. `0.74x` vertical multiplier (`1.15 / 0.74 = 1.55`), plus late-spray bimanual lateral oscillation (`Mathf.Sin(_sdnPhase * 2.2f) * 0.65f * Mathf.Max(0f, _sprayHeat - 0.25f)`) so sustained sprays weave left-right across the target's torso/shoulders rather than tracing a robotic vertical line.

4. **Dual-Layer Pathfinding Architecture (`TacticalBotBrain.cs` + `BotController.cs`):**
   - **Layer 1 (Strategic Spatial Selection — `TacticalBotBrain` @ ~4 Hz):** Selects the world destination (`CoverPoint` LOS-hidden scoring $-d - 30\cdot\mathbb{I}[d_T<6] + \text{rng}$, `TryPickFlank` $90^\circ$ off-axis tangent scoring, `PickPatrol` staleness/weight/distance scoring, `FlagZoneController` 8-slot core/outer ring, and `1.5–4.5 s` Hunt corner-slicing pause).
   - **Layer 2 (Local NavMesh Corridor & Kinematic Steering — `BotController` @ every frame):** Decoupled `NavMeshAgent` (`updatePosition = false`, `updateRotation = false`) queried with `0.6 s` / `1 m` hysteresis; extracts `Agent.steeringTarget` planar tangent; slices approaching corners (`Agent.path.corners[1]`) while sweeping `±28°`; blends combat ADAD strafing (`0.65 * route + 0.85 * right * strafeDir`); completes OffMeshLinks; and triggers `1.1 s` mantle-jump / `2.4 s` `ResetPath()` stuck recovery.
