# B32 - War-torn but classy UI typography

## Recommendation

Use **Saira Stencil One** for the Lead Rush II title, chapter cards, and large menu headings.
Pair it with **Barlow Condensed** for HUD labels, weapon/ammo readouts, objective text, and
compact menu controls. Saira Stencil One replaces the current Bebas/Bebas-Neue-derived title
look with a genuinely stencil-cut identity while retaining enough open counters for menu
readability. Keep Barlow Condensed for the HUD rather than using the stencil face for
information text.

The recommendation is a visual direction, not a claim that either face is the exact shipped
font of a particular AAA game. Current military-shooter UI conventions are a useful reference:
condensed display lettering for faction/title identity, and an open, high-x-height sans for
information that must be read while moving. Critical HUD labels should be tested at the
project's target 1080p viewing distance; a practical starting point is 20-22 px for critical
labels, then a visual test at the smallest supported resolution.

## Shortlist

| Font | Intended role | Why it fits | Risks / implementation notes | License |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Bebas Neue](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/bebasneue) | Title/display | Tall condensed capitals, strong poster/briefing character, clean silhouette | All-caps title only; weak choice for paragraphs and tiny HUD values | SIL OFL 1.1 |
| [Anton](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/anton) | Title/display | Heavy, blunt condensed strokes read as forceful and contemporary | Can overpower a busy background; use generous tracking and short strings | SIL OFL 1.1 |
| [Oswald](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/oswald) | Display or HUD headings | Narrow, disciplined grotesk with multiple weights | Narrow weights need extra tracking for all-caps labels | SIL OFL 1.1 |
| [Barlow Condensed](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/barlowcondensed) | HUD and compact controls | Open counters, useful weight range, compact width without stencil gimmicks | Condensed body copy needs a minimum size and line spacing; do not use Light for HUD | SIL OFL 1.1 |
| [Rajdhani](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/rajdhani) | HUD/telemetry accent | Technical, squared forms suggest equipment readouts while remaining readable | Distinctive shapes can make long prose tiring; reserve for labels and numbers | SIL OFL 1.1 |
| [Saira Condensed](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/sairacondensed) | HUD/body alternative | Broad weight range and neutral industrial tone; works in sentence case | Less dramatic for the title; needs contrast or tracking in large headings | SIL OFL 1.1 |
| [Teko](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/teko) | Title/display alternative | Very compact vertical rhythm and strong faction-marker presence | Too compressed for body text; avoid long words and small sizes | SIL OFL 1.1 |

## Readability rules for Gemini's TMP assets

1. Keep title faces to short strings, mostly title case or all caps; do not use Bebas Neue,
   Anton, Teko, or Rajdhani for paragraphs.
2. Use Barlow Condensed Regular/Medium for HUD text, with Semibold/Bold for values and
   warnings. Avoid Light weights over HDRP scenery.
3. Keep critical HUD labels at or above 20 px at 1080p during the first pass, then test the
   smallest supported resolution and a motion-heavy firefight screenshot.
4. Prefer sentence case for instructions and objective descriptions. All caps is reserved
   for short labels (`AMMO`, `RELOAD`, `OBJECTIVE`) and needs added tracking.
5. Test `I 1 l O 0`, `S 5`, punctuation, weapon names, and the full hitmarker vocabulary.
   The font must remain distinguishable with the game's red/amber/white HUD palette.
6. Use a dark translucent backing or local shadow/outline rather than a heavy glow; this
   preserves the war-torn look without blooming against bright maps.

## Public-repository license requirements

All shortlisted families are linked to their official Google Fonts source directories, which
include their `OFL.txt` and metadata. The SIL Open Font License permits use, embedding,
modification, and redistribution, but the font software and modified font software must
remain under the OFL and cannot be sold by itself. Gemini should copy the chosen font's
`OFL.txt` and copyright notice into the repository's third-party notices when adding the
binary/TMP assets. Do not mix in a similarly named commercial "military" font or a download
from an unverified font mirror.

Sources:

- [Google Fonts repository](https://github.com/google/fonts), official family source and
  metadata directories linked above.
- [SIL OFL FAQ](https://openfontlicense.org/ofl-faq/), redistribution, embedding, and
  modified-font requirements.
- [SIL OFL 1.1 text](https://openfontlicense.org/documents/OFL-1.1.txt), the license to ship
  with any redistributed font software.
- [Apple Typography guidance](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/typography),
  reference for size, hierarchy, and text-style testing.
- [Game UI type-system guide](https://www.sidebearings.com/game-ui-type-system/), practical
  discussion of HUD legibility, optical sizing, and viewing-distance constraints.

## Handoff

Create TMP assets for **Saira Stencil One** (title) and **Barlow Condensed** (HUD/body
controls) first. Keep the current LR2_Bebas/Barlow assets as fallbacks during the migration
and compare the same menu, objective, weapon, and hitmarker strings at 1080p and the minimum
target resolution. Gemini should mock the title in both Saira Stencil One and Big Shoulders
Stencil before committing the asset set.
