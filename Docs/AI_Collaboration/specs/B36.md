# B36 - Realistic, Gritty, War-Torn Music & Game Audio Specification

## 1. Goal and Problem Statement

`Lead Rush II` currently synthesizes procedural music stems (`Music_Menu`, `Music_Calm`, `Music_Tension`, `Music_Combat`) driven by a 3-stem `LR2MusicDirector` and mixes weapon, foley, voice, and ambience channels through `LR2Audio`. While sample-locked stem transitions and HDR-inspired ducking already exist, the mix and compositions need deeper **gritty, war-torn realism and dynamic grandness**:
1. Music stems should evoke battered military tension (low orchestral cello/contrabass drones, dusty field-brass fifths, industrial anvil/chassis hits, distant muffled artillery thuds, and subtle analog tape/radio grit in D Phrygian) rather than clean synth arpeggios, while keeping upper-mid transient bands (`4.5-8 kHz`) clear so footsteps (`-15.9 dB` under weapons, `B5`) and pain vocals remain readable.
2. Weapon environmental tails (`specs/P1.md`) must route consistently through `WeaponSoundTailRouter` with capped overlap (`<= 4` simultaneous tails, `45 ms` minimum tail gap), distinct indoor reverberant decay vs. dry outdoor reflections (`+3 to +6 dB` tail contrast indoors without inflating close muzzle transient peak), and scene-wide warzone depth (distant artillery rumble & radio/wind texture bed).

## 3. Architectural Design

### 3.1 Music Stem Upgrade (`LR2MusicGenerator.cs` + `LR2MusicDirector.cs`)
- Retain `16.0 s` seamless circular stems at `44.1 kHz` in **D Minor / D Phrygian** (`120 BPM`, 8 bars) so `PlayScheduled` sample alignment remains zero-click.
- **Music_Menu (Briefing / Aftermath):**
  - Replace bright bell arpeggios with low bowed war-cello fifths (`D2/A2/Eb3`), dusty analog radio/vinyl bed filtered below `1.9 kHz`, sparse metallic sonar chimes, and slow brass horn swells in bars 4-7 with subtle asymmetrical waveshaping (`Tanh`).
- **Music_Calm (Reconnaissance):**
  - Keep upper-mid (`> 3.6 kHz`) cleanly sculpted below `-18 dB` relative to RMS so `Footsteps` (`0.16x`, `-15.9 dB` vs gunfire) remain unmistakably clear.
  - Add muted distant warfare bed (ultra-low filtered rumble impulses below `160 Hz` mimicking far-off artillery at `~0.15x` bar intervals) + bowed contrabass pulse.
- **Music_Tension (Contact Imminent):**
  - Layer Phrygian flat-2 chromatic tension (`Eb` half-step pull against `D`), gritty saturated palm-muted riffing, tight metallic frame drums (`60-115 Hz`), and tense tremolo strings.
- **Music_Combat (Heavy Firefight):**
  - Full war-torn climax: driving orchestral bass/brass power fifths, heavy industrial metallic anvil/chassis strikes alongside combat kick/snare, aggressive tube-overdriven guitar ostinato (`MixProgressiveGuitar`), and rolling snare/tom turnarounds while soft-knee limiting (`maxPeak <= 0.86f`) prevents digital clipping.
- **Runtime Director (`LR2MusicDirector.cs`):**
  - Keep `B58` non-ducking, voice-protected ceilings (`menuCeiling = 0.24f`, `calmCeiling = 0.15f`, `tensionCeiling = 0.26f`, `combatCeiling = 0.38f`, `priority = 8`, `bypassEffects/bypassListenerEffects/bypassReverbZones = true`) so gunfire never silences combat music while footsteps remain readable (`-15.9 dB` under weapons).
  - Add a procedural gritty war-torn low-brass / sub-impact combat surge stinger (`< 950 Hz`, priority `10`, `10 s` cooldown) triggered when firefights escalate into heavy combat (`_targetIntensity >= 0.72f` from lower tension) or nearby explosions/kills, complementing the snare drumroll cue without adding treble fatigue.

### 3.2 Environmental Tails & Warfare Ambience (`LR2Audio.cs` + `AmbientOneShots.cs` + `P1` Hook)
- Wire a pooled `WeaponSoundTailRouter` instance inside `LR2Audio.EnsureRoot()` and expose `LR2Audio.PlayWeaponTail(...)`:
  - Checks `WeaponSoundTailRouter.CanPlay(Time.unscaledTime)` (`maxOverlappingTails = 4`, `minimumTailGap = 0.045f`) so automatic `600 RPM` gunfire cannot stack a muddy tail wall of sound.
  - Scales gain by `WeaponSoundTailRouter.Gain(indoors)` (`1.0f` indoors vs `0.65f` outdoors -> `3.74 dB` separation matching `specs/P1.md`).
  - Tracks active tail reservations per `AudioSource` (`ActiveTailVoices`) so both rejected voice allocations (`B65`) AND stolen active voices (`B61` `A26`) immediately release their tail reservation (`CancelReservation` / `NotifyTailEnded`).
  - Upgrades `LR2Audio.IsIndoors(Vector3)` to a direction-independent 5-ray hemisphere canopy probe (straight up + 4 compass diagonals at 30 deg from vertical, `22 m` against `EnvironmentMask`: roof overhead + >= 2 enclosed diagonals) so doorways and south-facing interiors classify accurately.
- Add war-torn horizon ambience in `AmbientOneShots.cs`:
  - Lazily synthesizes two low-band warzone horizon beds (`WarzoneArtilleryRumble` `< 260 Hz` far-off multi-boom barrage and `WarzoneMetalWindCreak` `160-950 Hz` dusty structural wind groan) played spatially `42-90 m` around the listener under `AudioCategory.Ambience` (automatically ducked by local gunfire HDR `DuckFactor`, zero high-frequency hiss over footsteps).

## 4. Acceptance Criteria
1. Both `LR2.Runtime.csproj` and `LR2.Editor.csproj` compile with `0` warnings and `0` errors.
2. Regenerated stems in `Assets/LR2/Resources/LR2/Music/` preserve seamless loop boundaries (`|sample[0] - sample[N-1]| < 0.005`) and strict RMS hierarchy (`Calm < Tension < Combat`).
3. `Footsteps` remain `14-18 dB` under `Weapons` (`stepGain=0.160`, `-15.9 dB`) during gameplay QA runs, `MenuSFX` stays non-extreme, and `LR2MusicDirector` telemetry transitions smoothly across `Calm -> Tension -> Combat`.
4. `LR2Audio.PlayWeaponTail` caps concurrent overlapping tails at `4` with distinct indoor (`1.0x`) vs outdoor (`0.65x`) gain.
