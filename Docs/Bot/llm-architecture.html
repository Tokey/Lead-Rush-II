No Jargon Version: Four Types of Bot Brain in Lead Rush II

Every match in Lead Rush II pits human reflexes against one of four bot archetypes you can now mix freely in the new COMBAT LOBBY (SKIRMISH | EXPERIMENTS):

1 · HumanLike [Rule AI]

Fast 60 Hz Human Kinematics

Runs 100% locally in C# with zero internet calls. Reacts slower out of the corner of its eye, whips fast mouse flicks (Fitts's Law), compensates recoil slightly late, and weaves ADAD left-right in firefights.

2 · DumbBot [Control]

Matched K/D, Zero Human Habits

Built specifically as a Turing-test benchmark. Matches HumanLike K/D (0.96 vs 1.04 Veteran) using rigid constant-speed aiming, stepped accuracy cones, zero crouching, zero sprinting, and zero jiggle peeks.

3 · LLM Enhanced [Groq]

Two-Brain Coach + Reflex Loop

System 2 (Groq cloud LLM) acts as tactical coach every ~8–10 seconds using fair fog-of-war intel (no wallhacks). System 1 (60 Hz C# muscle code) still aims and pulls the trigger every frame so gunplay never lags.

4 · HumanFitted [Your Data]

Equation-Fitted Clone

Reads Data/Profiles/human_behavior_fit.json fitted by Tools/analyze_experiment_session.py from your own gameplay CSVs—matching your reaction curve, flick speed, pull-down gain, and crouch/strafe cadence.

04 · Data-Driven Humanization & Experiment Workflow

    
Closing the Loop With Your Own Experiment Telemetry

    

      

        
        

          
          
        

      

      

        

          
1. Design Your Session in COMBAT LOBBY → EXPERIMENTS

          
Pick the 8-MATCH: 4 HUMAN vs 4 LLM or 6-MATCH 1V1 BLIND TURING preset, mix HUMAN, DUMB, LLM, and FITTED bots per slot, toggle BLIND ON, and rate each match on the post-game Turing scale.

        

        

          
2. Zero-Stutter Export to Data/Logs/<sessionId>_<runId>/

          
All ticks (20/30/60 Hz), flicks, recoil bursts, events, and hardware/frame-delivery audits buffer in pre-allocated structs and flush on a background thread behind the obsidian SAVING EXPERIMENT TELEMETRY screen.

        

        

          
3. Mine & Clone Your Playstyle Into HumanFitted

          
Run python Tools/analyze_experiment_session.py --session Data/Logs/<id>_<runId>, then copy human_behavior_fit.json to Data/Profiles/human_behavior_fit.json so the game's FITTED bots adopt your exact Fitts curve, Beta speed skew, strafe Hz, and recoil pull-down.
